General Instructions

    Navigating the Test

    • Click an option to select it. For numerical questions, type your answer in the box. Responses are saved automatically.
    • Save & Next moves to the next question. Clear Response removes your answer to the current question.
    • Mark for Review & Next flags a question so you can come back to it later.
    • Use the Question Palette to jump to any question at any time.
    • A Submit Test button is available on every question. You will see a summary before final submission.

    Question Palette colour codes:

    1 Not Visited
    2 Not Answered
    3 Answered
    4 Marked for Review
    5 Answered & Marked for Review

    Questions that are Answered & Marked for Review will be evaluated. Questions only marked for review (without an answer) will not.
