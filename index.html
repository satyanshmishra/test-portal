General Instructions

    Navigating the Test

    • Click an option to select it. For numerical questions, type your answer in the box. Responses are saved automatically.
    • Save & Next moves to the next question. Clear Response removes your answer to the current question.
    • Mark for Review & Next flags a question so you can come back to it later.
    • Use the Question Palette to jump to any question at any time.
    • A Submit Test button is available on every question. You will see a summary before final submission.

    Question Palette colour codes:

    1 Not Visited
    2 Not Answered
    3 Answered
    4 Marked for Review
    5 Answered & Marked for Review

    Questions that are Answered & Marked for Review will be evaluated. Questions only marked for review (without an answer) will not.

    Terms & Conditions

    1. I am the student who owns this account, and I will attempt this test myself.
    2. I will not use any books, notes, calculators, mobile phones, websites, AI tools or help from any other person during the test.
    3. I understand that the timer cannot be paused once the test has started, and it keeps running even if I close or leave this page.
    4. I understand that this paper can be attempted only once from my account.
    5. I understand that the test will be submitted automatically when the time limit ends.
    6. I understand that once I click Final Submit, I cannot change any answer.
    8. I understand that my name, email, mobile number, answers and result will be recorded by the teacher.
    9. I understand that the result and topic analysis are for practice and self-assessment only, and are not an official score.
    10. I will not copy, share or distribute the questions or solutions of this paper without permission.
